Hyderabad To Srisailam Tour Package

Hyderabad To Srisailam Tour Package

April 26, 2025 /Posted byVenkatesh / 1125 / 0

Embark on a soul-enriching journey from Hyderabad to Srisailam with our exclusive 1 Day Tour Package. Srisailam, nestled in the serene Nallamala Hills of Andhra Pradesh, is a sacred destination known for its temples, pristine landscapes, and spiritual aura. Discover the divinity of this place with our meticulously crafted itinerary, covering the following attractions:

1.

Mallikarjuna Temple

Begin your spiritual quest by visiting the Mallikarjuna Temple, dedicated to Lord Shiva. This ancient shrine boasts intricate architecture and holds immense religious significance.

2.

Bramarambika Devi Temple

Next, pay your respects at the Bramarambika Devi Temple, dedicated to the Goddess Parvati. The temple’s serene surroundings and architectural beauty will leave you mesmerized.

3.

Sakshi Ganapathi Temple: Seek blessings at the Sakshi Ganapathi Temple, where Lord Ganesha is believed to bear witness to the pilgrims’ devotion. The tranquil ambiance here is perfect for introspection.

4.

Paladhara, Panchadhara

Immerse yourself in the divine energy of the Paladhara and Panchadhara, natural springs revered for their purifying properties. A dip here is believed to cleanse the soul.

5.

Hatkeshwar Temple

Visit the Hatkeshwar Temple, dedicated to Lord Shiva, and admire the intricate carvings and architecture that reflect the rich cultural heritage of the region.

6.

Srisailam Dam

Explore the Srisailam Dam, a majestic engineering marvel set against the backdrop of lush greenery. The view of the dam against the serene waters of the Krishna River is breathtaking.

7.

Paatala Ganga

Descend to the Paatala Ganga, a sacred river believed to originate from the foot of Lord Mallikarjuna. The peaceful surroundings make it a perfect spot for reflection.

8.

Octopus View Point

End your day with a visit to the Octopus View Point, which offers panoramic views of the Srisailam reservoir and its surroundings. Capture the beauty of the landscape in your memories.
